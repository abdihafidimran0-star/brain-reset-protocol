4:15AM
Wake — Wudu — Tahajjud
Phone in the other room. Rise, make wudu, stand in the dark. Pray as many raka'at as the time allows — minimum 4. Then sit in istighfar and du'a until Fajr enters. This is not productivity time — it is presence time.

4:15AM
Istighfar + Du'a until Fajr
After Tahajjud, remain in a state of remembrance. Istighfar, salawat, personal du'a. No phone, no input. The window between Tahajjud and Fajr is one of the most accepted times for du'a.

Fajr 5:20 → 5:55
5:20AM
Salat al-Fajr (Adhan → Iqamah → Salah)
Pray at the adhan. Two sunnah raka'at first, then the fard. Do not rush out of sajdah. The quality of this prayer sets the tone of the entire day.
Deen
5:40AM
Adhkar al-Sabah — Full, Not Rushed
Every du'a in full. No skipping. Adhkar al-sabah is the brain's spiritual firewall — it is not optional and it is not decorative. Finish by 5:55.
Deen
Qur'an Block 6:00 → 6:30
6:00AM
Muraja'ah — 3-Tier System (30 min)
Brain is at peak sharpness. World is silent. No phone has been touched yet. This is the most protected Qur'an block of the day — give it everything.
Tier 1 — Strong
10 min · Rotation of mastered juz. Fluent pace, ears open.
Tier 2 — Weak
13 min · Shaky juz — slow, deliberate, repeat to lock in.
Tier 3 — New
7 min · Recent hifz consolidation. Small portions, multiple passes.
Qur'an
